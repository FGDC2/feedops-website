The score is a starting point — not the destination.

A higher score is not automatically better if it comes from investing time in low-value products. Ask whether the products and categories being improved can contribute meaningfully to revenue, profit or customer acquisition.

Recommendations and status

Use Google’s recommendations to ask better questions.

The report may highlight Local Inventory Ads, price and availability mismatches, local offer eligibility, approvals, impressions or click-through rate. Treat these as areas to investigate rather than instructions to follow blindly.

Stage 1

Maximise Total Offers

Make more of your products available across Google’s shopping surfaces. In Merchant Center, an “offer” is an individual product listing supplied through your Google Shopping feed that Google can review, match and potentially show to shoppers. Maximising total offers means ensuring that as much of your relevant catalogue as possible is eligible to appear wherever customers search and discover products.

Availability before advertising

Submit the opportunity broadly.

The objective is not simply to submit more products. It is to make the right products available in the right places, with accurate pricing, availability, imagery and optimised product information.

A larger catalogue does not automatically create better results. Products still need to be approved, in stock, relevant to your customers and commercially worthwhile to promote.

Stage 2

Increase approval rate, starting with important products

Disapproved products and Merchant Center errors can prevent impressions or clicks, so approval is one of the foundations of product-feed performance. But not every disapproved product deserves the same level of effort.

Common approval issues

Start by locating the type of failure.

Live product data

Price or availability mismatches

Identifiers and content

Invalid GTINs, missing attributes or poor imagery

Website and fulfilment

Landing-page access, structured data or shipping

Local inventory

Missing or inconsistent store availability data

The most common approval issues

Keep the feed, website and inventory systems aligned.

Price and availability mismatches often occur when the website updates before the feed, inventory systems are not synchronised or sale prices change without the product data being refreshed.

Automatic item updates can correct occasional differences, but they are a safety net—not a substitute for reliable, frequent feed updates.

Invalid GTINs should be corrected where possible. If an identifier is unavailable or uncertain, it is safer to omit it than submit an incorrect one.

Generic images, promotional overlays and placeholder images can also lead to disapprovals or reduced visibility. Replace them with genuine, accurate product imagery, prioritising high-value products first.

Shipping information, structured data and local inventory must also match what customers see on the website and in stores.

Stage 3

Increase targeted offers without advertising every eligible product

A product can be approved, in stock and eligible to appear on Google, but still be excluded from active advertising campaigns. That does not mean it should be removed from Merchant Center.

Control advertising with custom labels

Turn business priorities into product groups.

Labels can include or exclude products from Google Shopping campaigns without removing them from Merchant Center—and can update dynamically as the business changes.

The commercial question

“Are these products deliberately excluded, or have they simply been missed?”

There may be good reasons not to advertise every product. Low margin, unreliable stock, poor conversion, high return rates or unsuitable delivery costs may not justify paid investment.

But important products that are approved, in stock and receiving no advertising activity may represent a missed opportunity.

Local Inventory Ads

Turn nearby availability into intent.

For retailers with physical stores, local ads can show “in store”, distance, availability, click and collect, pickup timing, opening hours and directions.

This is more than another placement. It gives a shopper a reason to choose you over an online-only competitor and can support both store visits and online sales.

The opportunity depends on accurate store data, reliable inventory and products customers genuinely want locally.

02

The strength of the offer

Make value attractive and sustainable.

Competitive pricing matters, but value can also come from delivery speed, service, availability, returns, ratings and a complete proposition.

Commercial test

A promotion that raises CTR but destroys margin is not a successful result. Free shipping can help conversion while creating unacceptable fulfilment costs.

Promotions, shipping and returns

Use promotions to clear stock, grow a priority category, support peak trading, increase order value, move shoppers to a higher-margin product or compete for an important search. Ensure product data accurately reflects every promise.

Product and seller ratings

If ratings are missing, investigate technical eligibility, review-feed integration, product matching, volume, market eligibility and seller-rating requirements. The issue may be technical, operational or simply insufficient review volume.

Frequently asked questions

Merchant Center Excellence Report FAQs

The short answers to the questions retailers most often ask when deciding what to do with the report.

What is the Google Merchant Center Excellence report?

The report summarises opportunities across product eligibility, approvals, targeting, impressions, clicks and feed-based campaign activity. Treat it as a diagnostic starting point—not an official Google Ads performance score.

Does a higher Merchant Excellence Score guarantee better sales?

No. A higher score is only useful when the improvements involve commercially important products and contribute to relevant visibility, profitable traffic, conversion or customer acquisition.

Which Merchant Center issues should I fix first?

Prioritise problems affecting products with strong demand, healthy margins, reliable stock, active campaigns or strategic importance. High-value disapprovals, price and availability mismatches and missing inventory data should generally come before low-impact catalogue cleanup.

Should every eligible product be included in paid advertising?

No. Accurate eligible products can remain available in Merchant Center for free and organic discovery while campaigns, product groups and custom labels control which products receive paid media investment.

How should I use Demand Gen with a product feed?

Use Demand Gen selectively for products that match the audience, creative and commercial objective, such as new products, seasonal ranges, high-margin products or visually strong products that need more consideration before purchase.

What does an offer mean in Google Merchant Center?

An offer is an individual product listing that Google can review, match and potentially show to shoppers across eligible paid, free and local shopping surfaces.

Why can an approved product still receive few or no impressions?

Approval confirms that a product can participate, but visibility also depends on targeting, availability, relevance, competition, campaign coverage and the quality and completeness of the product data. Use the Google Shopping ads troubleshooting guide to work through those causes.

How can product feed data improve Google Shopping impressions?

Relevant titles, descriptions, product types, imagery and category-specific attributes can help Google understand the product and match it to more suitable searches. Approval, availability and campaign coverage must also be considered.

Which product attributes should retailers prioritise?

Prioritise attributes that influence search and comparison in the product category. Apparel may need size, colour and material, while electronics may depend more on brand, model, compatibility and technical specifications.

How often should the Merchant Center Excellence report be reviewed?

Review it regularly and after significant catalogue, website, inventory or campaign changes. Allow enough time for feed updates and Google processing before judging the effect of completed fixes.

Does better product data help AI shopping and agentic commerce?

Clear, accurate and structured product data helps AI-led systems understand what a product is, who it suits, its compatibility, specifications, price, availability and delivery options. It supports product understanding but does not guarantee recommendation or visibility.
