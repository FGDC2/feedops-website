FeedOps Channels FAQ

Feed Distribution & Channel Management

Do I need a separate feed for each channel?

No. FeedOps ingests your catalog once, then syndicates channel-perfect versions to Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, TikTok, Pinterest, and more—each tailored to platform format, compliance rules, and ranking logic.

Can FeedOps help with affiliate or partner feeds?

Yes. We deliver clean, always-current feeds to affiliates and comparison-shopping engines, keeping branding, pricing, and availability consistent across partner sites.

Can FeedOps support us on marketplaces like Amazon and eBay?

Yes. FeedOps formats and enriches marketplace feeds to boost visibility, search rank, and buy-box performance, while syncing inventory and shipping data to prevent oversells.

Does FeedOps handle order syncing from marketplaces?

Yes. Orders from Amazon, eBay, and other platforms flow back into your ecommerce system automatically, so you manage fulfilment in one place.

Shopping Ads & Local Inventory

How does FeedOps optimise Shopping Ads?

We rewrite titles, enrich attributes, and auto-resolve Merchant Center errors—lifting visibility and ROAS in Google Shopping, Performance Max, and Microsoft Ads.

Can FeedOps help us stay compliant with Google and Microsoft policies?

Yes. Self-healing rules fix most issues automatically; any manual fixes are flagged for you—maintaining full delivery eligibility.

How does FeedOps support Local Inventory Ads and store feeds?

We build store-specific feeds with local pricing, stock, and pickup info, then sync to Google and Microsoft LIA programs—and even local-targeted social ads.

Can I manage store-level pricing or inventory with FeedOps?

Yes. Per-store SKUs, pricing, and promos are supported. Syncs run as often as hourly, keeping data accurate in real time.

Social, Discovery & Email Channels

How is FeedOps different for Social and Discovery Ads?

FeedOps creates dynamic, enriched product data for Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Google Discovery, and Microsoft Audience Ads—so each platform’s AI can build scroll-stopping creatives automatically.

Can FeedOps manage content across organic and paid social campaigns?

Yes. Unified rules keep price, availability, and creative details in sync across ads and unpaid discovery surfaces.

What does FeedOps do for email marketing?

We connect your feed to platforms like Klaviyo, Mailchimp, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud, auto-populating newsletters and automations with live products, prices, and promos.

Does FeedOps handle order syncing from marketplaces?

Yes. Orders from Amazon, eBay, and other marketplaces sync back to your ecommerce platform automatically for streamlined fulfilment.

AI & Next-Gen Search

Does FeedOps optimise for emerging AI channels like ChatGPT or Google Gemini?

Yes. FeedOps restructures titles, descriptions, and metadata in conversational language with rich trust signals, helping products rank in AI-powered search tools such as Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, and ChatGPT shopping plugins.
