Channel fit

Is Lasoo right for your business?

Lasoo is well suited to retailers that sell to Australian consumers, can ship reliably across Australia and offer competitive pricing.

  • Have a broad or frequently changing product catalogue
  • Regularly run promotions or competitive offers
  • Want another source of marketplace discovery and sales
  • Can fulfil orders reliably across Australia or selected regions
  • Have sufficient margin to support marketplace costs
  • Want to test the channel with a controlled product assortment

Lasoo questions

Selling on Lasoo: frequently asked questions

What is Lasoo?

Lasoo is an Australian marketplace focused on products, promotions and better-value shopping. It allows consumers to discover, compare and purchase offers from multiple retailers across a broad range of categories.

Is Lasoo only a digital catalogue website?

No. Lasoo has evolved from its digital catalogue heritage into a transactional marketplace. It now supports direct product discovery and marketplace purchasing.

How does Lasoo rank in Australia?

For July 2026, Similarweb’s Australian marketplace rankings placed Lasoo at number 26, with approximately 92% of its traffic coming from Australia.

These are third-party estimates and will change over time.

How much does it cost to sell on Lasoo?

Lasoo does not appear to publish a standard public seller price list. Fees, commissions and other commercial terms should be confirmed directly with Lasoo.

FeedOps pricing covers the agreed product-data integration and management service and is separate from marketplace charges.

Can any retailer sell on Lasoo?

Retailers must meet Lasoo’s current seller, product and operational requirements. Lasoo makes the final decision regarding seller acceptance.

FeedOps can help assess and prepare your catalogue, but we cannot guarantee marketplace approval.

Does FeedOps integrate with Lasoo?

FeedOps can prepare and manage product data for Lasoo. The current connection method and available technical features will be confirmed during onboarding. We do not claim a direct API connection unless that capability has been technically verified.

Who fulfils Lasoo orders?

Products sold through marketplaces such as Lasoo are generally fulfilled by the retailer. Precise responsibilities for delivery, returns and customer service are established in the retailer’s agreement with Lasoo.

Can we choose which products are sent to Lasoo?

Yes. FeedOps can apply catalogue rules to include or exclude products based on agreed criteria such as category, brand, availability, price, margin or fulfilment capability.

Can we begin with only part of our catalogue?

Yes. Starting with a controlled assortment may be a sensible way to test the channel before expanding. FeedOps can create and maintain the inclusion rules for the agreed range.

Does FeedOps guarantee sales on Lasoo?

No. Marketplace results depend on product demand, pricing, competition, promotions, availability, fulfilment and the marketplace itself.

FeedOps improves and maintains the product-data foundation required to operate the channel effectively, but it cannot guarantee sales.
