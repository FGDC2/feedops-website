Google Shopping guide

Prepare your product data for conversational Shopping.

Conversational shopping experiences depend on product information that Google can understand and verify. This guide explains how FeedOps can map your configured product data into validated conversational attributes while preserving reliable feed behaviour.

Why conversational attributes matter.

Google describes conversational attributes as optional product data that can help AI systems and conversational agents understand your products’ specific nuances. They complement your existing Merchant Center data; they do not replace the core product specification.

Google’s Shopping Graph explainer shows why this context matters: Google uses product information such as colours, sizes, materials, reviews and availability to understand products and answer more specific shopping needs.

Google has also published a retailer example—not a full case study—where conversational attributes submitted by lululemon were incorporated 50% of the time in relevant AI Mode product recommendations during testing, providing shoppers with more precise answers. Treat this as Google-reported testing evidence, not a guaranteed performance benchmark.

Read Google’s conversational attributes guidance for the current field definitions and submission options.

Important: richer product information can help Google understand products, but it does not guarantee rankings, traffic, inclusion, or visibility in any particular conversational experience.

Step 1 · Start with the audit

See what to add, then map what you already have.

Use the FeedOps audit to identify which conversational attributes are recommended but missing. Then map the product information already available on your website into those attributes before asking the playbook to enrich it.

1. Check recommendations

Review the audit for conversational attributes that could improve your product data but are not yet present.

2. Map your website data

Map the fields you already have, including built-in platform fields and any custom fields that contain useful product or variant information.

3. Confirm the rules

Agree which products qualify, how values are normalised, and what happens when information is missing, duplicated or conflicting.

Websites connected through supported platform APIs, such as Shopify, BigCommerce and Magento, automatically provide available built-in fields. You can also map custom fields from your website.

Step 2 · Roll out

Audit, map, enrich, then check the audit again.

  1. Review your FeedOps audit and note the conversational attributes recommended for your products.
  2. Map the relevant built-in or custom fields from your website into those attributes.
  3. Use the FeedOps playbook to enrich the mapped product facts into structured conversational content.
  4. Review the generated feed output and resolve any mapping or content issues.
  5. Recheck the FeedOps audit to confirm the recommended attributes are now present and valid.
  6. Monitor output quality as you roll out and expand the approach across more products.

FeedOps playbook

Use the playbook to enrich the mapped facts.

Once your website data is mapped, the FeedOps playbook makes it more useful without inventing product facts. It identifies useful questions, writes concise answers from approved evidence, standardises group titles, and expresses variant options consistently across a product family.

Questions and answers

Identify the questions shoppers are likely to ask, then answer them from approved product facts, specifications, documentation or seller expertise.

Item group titles

Create a clear shared title for a product family, preserving the group identity while keeping individual product titles and IDs intact.

Variant options

Normalise the option name and value for each variant—such as colour, size, memory or width—so Google can distinguish the choices within the group.

Google’s example shows these fields working together: an item group title identifies the product family, variant options describe the specific version, and question-and-answer pairs provide decision-making context.

Supported attributes

Conversational attributes you can prepare.

  • question_and_answer: product-specific questions and answers.
  • item_group_title: a shared title for products in the same variant group.
  • variant_option: the option represented by a particular variant.
  • document_link: approved product documentation links.
  • related_product: configured complementary or alternative products.
  • popularity_rank: an agreed ranking signal with a defined scale and freshness expectation.

Validation first

Safe degradation is part of the design.

Optional conversational attributes should not make existing feed generation fail. Missing, invalid, conflicting, duplicate or out-of-range values need explicit rules. Depending on the attribute, the correct outcome may be a normalised value, a safe fallback, or omission with an audit trail.

  • Keep output deterministic and explainable from configured source data.
  • Do not guess missing product facts.
  • Confirm URL, product identity, relationship and range checks for the relevant attribute.
  • Keep unresolved edge cases as review items until the mapping specification is approved.

Step 3 · Recheck

Use the audit to confirm the rollout.

When your mapped fields have been enriched by the playbook, run the FeedOps audit again. Check that the recommended conversational attributes are now present, valid and supported by the product data on your website.

If an attribute is still missing, return to the mapping step and check whether the source field is available, whether a custom field needs to be mapped, or whether the product is eligible for that attribute.

Readiness checklist

Before you roll out.

  • The audit recommendations for your products are understood.
  • Relevant built-in and custom website fields are mapped.
  • Questions and answers, item group titles and variant options have been enriched from approved product data.
  • The generated feed output has been reviewed.
  • The audit has been rechecked and remaining gaps have an explanation or next step.
