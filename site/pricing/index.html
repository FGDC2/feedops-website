FAQs

Clear answers before you commit

How is FeedOps priced?

FeedOps generally provides a fixed monthly price under an annual agreement. Pricing reflects catalogue complexity, integrations, destinations, processing requirements and managed-service scope.

Does the price include the managed service?

Yes. Pricing covers both the platform and the specialist team responsible for managing the agreed operation.

Do you charge according to revenue or GMV?

No. Pricing is based on the technology, processing and specialist work required to deliver the agreed scope.

Do you charge per user?

Not ordinarily. Current plans support unlimited authorised customer and agency users.

Is there an onboarding fee?

Standard onboarding and standard integrations are ordinarily included. Bespoke integrations and non-standard work may cost extra.

What is the standard agreement term?

Larger managed-service engagements generally begin with a 12-month agreement, billed monthly in advance.

Can we begin with a pilot?

A structured pilot can be discussed where appropriate. Its scope, duration, success measures and commercial terms are agreed before commencement.

Why doesn’t FeedOps publish standard packages?

Each engagement varies by catalogue complexity, integrations, destinations, processing and support. Discovery produces a clear price for the actual scope.
