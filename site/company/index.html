Why FeedOps exists

As media became more automated, product data became more important

The founding team behind FeedOps worked together in a digital advertising agency supporting ecommerce retailers. As advertising platforms took control of more decisions through machine learning and predictive systems, the quality of the information entering those systems became increasingly important.

The insight

For ecommerce advertisers, product data was one of the most important inputs—and one of the few meaningful levers they could still control.

That insight led to FeedOps.

We built technology and a specialist service to help retailers keep their product data accurate, complete, relevant and ready for the systems deciding which products customers see.

What that means in practice

  • Healthy feeds provide the foundationReliable product information gives every destination a stronger starting point.
  • Better signals improve understandingStrong titles, attributes, taxonomy and commercial signals help platforms surface the right products.
  • Continuous improvement mattersFeedOps exists to manage and continually improve those inputs.
Every development since—from automated Shopping campaigns and Performance Max to paid social, marketplaces and AI-powered discovery—has reinforced that original belief.

How we work

Five principles guide every FeedOps engagement

  1. 01

    Own the operation

    We investigate issues, manage the work within our control and coordinate with the right stakeholders when another system is involved.

  2. 02

    Use rules before AI

    We map the information already available and apply reliable, repeatable rules. AI is introduced where deterministic methods cannot solve the problem efficiently.

  3. 03

    Connect data with commercial outcomes

    We improve product data to support stronger visibility, customer engagement and ecommerce performance.

  4. 04

    Keep customers in control

    Customers retain platform access, collaboration, approval control and ownership of their underlying data and generated content.

  5. 05

    Keep improving

    Catalogues, channels and customer behaviour keep changing. FeedOps provides the ongoing management needed to keep product information healthy, relevant and ready to perform.

Company FAQs

Questions about FeedOps

Clear answers about who we are, how our team works and where FeedOps fits within an ecommerce operation.

What is FeedOps?

FeedOps is an Australian product-data technology and managed-services company. We combine proprietary software with account managers, feed specialists and engineers who connect, improve, distribute and continually manage ecommerce product data.

Where is FeedOps based?

FeedOps is headquartered in Adelaide, South Australia, with team members located across Australia and India. We support ecommerce businesses operating across Australia, New Zealand and international markets.

Is FeedOps a software platform or a managed service?

It is both. Customers and their authorised agency partners receive access to the FeedOps platform, while our specialists take responsibility for the engineering, feed operations, monitoring and ongoing optimisation agreed within the engagement.

Who works with FeedOps customers?

Each managed engagement has a dedicated FeedOps Account Manager backed by feed specialists and technical support engineers. This provides one accountable contact with access to the broader expertise needed to manage complex product-data work.

What makes FeedOps different from a typical feed tool?

Most feed tools provide software and leave the customer or agency to operate it. FeedOps combines the platform with people who own the day-to-day work, coordinate across systems and teams, investigate issues and continually improve the product data.

Does FeedOps replace our ecommerce or media agency?

No. FeedOps works alongside internal ecommerce teams, technology partners and media agencies. We take ownership of the product-data layer so each partner can work from accurate, channel-ready information and stay focused on their own responsibilities.
