Quick answers

FeedOps in plain terms.

FeedOps is an Australian-led product feed optimisation platform and expert support service for ecommerce teams that need cleaner product data across shopping, marketplace, paid social, local inventory, and AI discovery channels.

01

What does FeedOps do?

FeedOps audits, manages, enriches, and improves ecommerce product feeds so products are easier for media channels, marketplaces, and discovery systems to understand.

02

Who is FeedOps for?

FeedOps is for ecommerce teams, media teams, agencies, and retailers whose product data affects Google Shopping, PMax, paid social, local inventory, marketplaces, or AI product discovery.

03

How is FeedOps different?

FeedOps combines feed management software, automated audits, feed rules, AI enrichment, platform access, and feed experts instead of only translating data between formats.

Why FeedOps exists

FeedOps came from the point where media strategy kept running into product data reality.

FeedOps started inside Dynamic Creative, a retail-focused media agency that worked deeply with Shopping campaigns, structured product data, and performance marketing. After years of seeing growth blocked by messy catalogues, missing attributes, approval issues, and feed gaps, the team built a more practical way to manage the feed layer.

When the agency was sold in 2022, FeedOps became the focus: a product feed management company built for ecommerce teams that need software, automation, and people who understand the commercial outcome.

  • Born from agency workBuilt by people who had to connect feed quality to campaign performance every day.
  • Focused on executionNot just reports. FeedOps helps diagnose, prioritise, and fix feed problems.
  • Designed to collaborateWorks with ecommerce teams, media teams, executives, and agency partners.
