Privacy policy

How FeedOps handles privacy, cookies, and platform data.

This page explains what information FeedOps collects, why we use it, how we protect it, and how you can ask us to access, correct, restrict, or delete personal information.

At a glance
  • We collect information needed to run the site, respond to enquiries, and provide FeedOps services.
  • We use cookies for site function and, with consent where required, analytics and marketing.
  • We use safeguards to protect platform and marketplace data.
  • You can contact us about privacy requests at any time.

What we collect

FeedOps may collect information you provide directly, information created when you use our website, and information needed to provide FeedOps products and services.

Contact detailsName, email address, phone number, company, job role, and enquiry details.
Website activityPages visited, browser type, device information, referring site, approximate location, and interaction data.
Service dataProduct catalogue information, feed settings, channel configuration, diagnostics, errors, and support history.

We collect personal information only where it is reasonably needed for the way you interact with FeedOps, such as requesting a demo, downloading resources, joining a webinar, using the platform, or asking for support.

How we use information

We use information to operate FeedOps, respond to requests, improve our website and services, and support ecommerce teams with product feed and channel work.

  • To respond to enquiries, demo requests, support requests, and account questions.
  • To provide access to FeedOps tools, reports, audits, guides, webinars, and platform features.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the FeedOps website and platform.
  • To understand which website content and product information is useful.
  • To send relevant product, service, or marketing communications where permitted.
  • To meet legal, security, marketplace, platform, and compliance obligations.

Cookies and consent choices

FeedOps uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the website working and to understand how people use our content. Where optional analytics or marketing cookies are used, the cookie notice lets visitors choose between necessary cookies only or accepting all cookies.

Necessary only

Necessary cookies help the site work, such as remembering a cookie choice, supporting forms, keeping sessions stable, and protecting basic security. They are not used for advertising or remarketing.

Accept all

Accept all allows necessary cookies plus optional analytics and marketing cookies. These can help us measure website performance, improve content, understand campaigns, and support future GTM consent settings.

When Google Tag Manager, analytics, conversion tracking, remarketing, or other optional tools are used, they should respect the visitor's saved consent choice.

Platform and marketplace data

FeedOps may process product, catalogue, feed, channel, marketplace, and diagnostic information so that we can provide feed management, product data optimisation, approval support, enrichment, reporting, and related services.

Amazon and marketplace data

Where FeedOps processes Amazon or other marketplace data, we use physical, administrative, and technical safeguards designed to protect that information. Data in transit is protected using HTTPS, and sensitive information is handled with access controls and retention controls appropriate to the service being provided.

  • Marketplace data is accessed only for authorised service, support, diagnostic, or operational purposes.
  • Access is limited to people and systems that need it to perform the work.
  • FeedOps does not intentionally retain marketplace personal information beyond the operational need for the service.
  • Security incidents involving marketplace data are escalated and handled through our incident response process.

When information may be shared

FeedOps does not sell personal information. We may share information where needed to operate the website, provide services, support customers, work with trusted service providers, comply with law, or protect FeedOps, our users, or others.

  • With service providers that help us run hosting, forms, CRM, analytics, communication, security, support, or platform operations.
  • With channel, marketplace, or platform providers where needed to connect, diagnose, optimise, or support product feeds.
  • With professional advisers, regulators, courts, or law enforcement where required or appropriate.
  • In connection with a business transaction, such as restructuring, merger, sale, or acquisition.

Security, retention, and deletion

FeedOps uses reasonable technical, administrative, and organisational safeguards to protect information from unauthorised access, misuse, loss, interference, disclosure, or alteration.

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to provide services, to meet legal or accounting obligations, to resolve disputes, to protect security, or to maintain business records. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete, de-identify, or securely dispose of it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request a copy of personal information we hold about you.

  • You can ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • You can ask us to delete or restrict certain personal information, subject to legal and operational limits.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing communications using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

We will respond to privacy requests within a reasonable period and may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Privacy questions, answered plainly

These short answers summarise the most common privacy questions about FeedOps, cookies, analytics, and data requests.

What personal information does FeedOps collect?

FeedOps may collect contact details, enquiry details, website activity, and service data needed to run the website, respond to requests, provide FeedOps services, and support product feed and marketplace workflows.

What is the difference between necessary cookies and accepting all cookies?

Necessary cookies help the website work, such as remembering a cookie choice, keeping forms working, and supporting basic security. Accepting all also allows optional analytics and marketing cookies.

Does FeedOps sell personal information?

No. FeedOps does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where needed to operate the website, provide services, work with trusted service providers, comply with law, or protect FeedOps and its users.
