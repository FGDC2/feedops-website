FeedOps Pricing FAQs

Pricing, Plans & Support

What are the FeedOps White-Glove plans?
  • Essentials – Ad channels and affiliate feeds (Google, Microsoft, Meta, TikTok).
  • Plus – Essentials + local inventory feeds, store-level campaigns, TikTok, Criteo, affiliate, email.
  • Complete – Plus + managed marketplace feeds (Amazon, eBay, etc.) and order/shipping sync.

VIP (invite-only) – Complete + direct C-suite access, bespoke AI models, and priority roadmap input.

What’s included in the 30-Day Pilot?

A production feed for one or more channels—usually Google—fully optimised with AI enrichment, error resolution, and alignment to your goals. It’s a real-world test, not a demo.

Is the pilot really refundable?

Yes. If FeedOps isn’t the right fit after 30 days, we refund the pilot fee—no pressure, no hassle.

What happens during the 30-Day Pilot?

We baseline KPIs (CTR, RPLI, error rate), run live optimisations, and review results with you. All changes stay in a separate feed until you approve them.

What happens after the challenge?

You move into the White-Glove plan that fits your growth goals. We continue proactive optimisation, channel expansion, and strategic support on a term agreement.

Do I need to use all channels right away?

No. Start with your highest-value channel—Google, Meta, Microsoft, etc.—and add more over time. Plans are built to grow with you.

Is there a separate platform licence fee?

No. Software, AI, and Concierge service are bundled into one monthly subscription.

Are support tickets or hours limited?

No. Every White-Glove tier includes unlimited tickets with same-day responses; no reasonable request is refused.

What happens to my feed if I don’t go ahead after the pilot?

Nothing is pushed live unless you continue. Your original feed stays unchanged; pilot optimisations remain unpublished, and you keep the insights.
