What it checks

Are your products being seen and clicked?

For your products to sell, they first need to be visible in Google Shopping. Once they are visible, they need to earn clicks. The audit looks at Google Merchant Center independently of Google Ads, so you can see whether product visibility and click signals are being held back by the feed itself.

Account compliance is the on or off switch for product visibility.

If your Merchant Center account is not compliant, products can be disapproved or the account can be suspended, which means visibility stops before media performance even has a chance. The audit checks the compliance signals that can affect whether products are eligible to show, and it also helps confirm that the programs you are eligible for are turned on so your products can appear in the places Google makes available.

If products are not approved, they cannot be seen.

Product approval shows which products are eligible for each Google surface, including Shopping ads, free listings, and display ads. The audit separates approval by channel so you can see where important products are blocked, where issues need attention, and whether the products that matter most are actually able to appear where shoppers can find them.

If attributes are incomplete, Google has less context for what you sell.

Attributes help Google understand, classify, match, and recommend your products across search, free listings, ads, and conversational commerce. The audit highlights weak or missing titles, product types, identifiers, variants, colours, sizes, materials, weights, and other product details that can limit visibility, searchability, and discoverability.

Other measures help you see what is working, what is being ignored, and what to do next.

Products with clicks

Find which visible products are actually earning traffic and shopper attention.

Spot products that get impressions but no clicks, so ignored products can be prioritised for review.

What does the audit check?

The audit checks signals such as visibility, products with impressions but no clicks, products with no impressions, attribute completeness, and product data gaps that may affect paid Shopping and free listings.

What are zombie products?

Zombie products are products that receive impressions but do not receive clicks. They may be visible, but they are not attracting enough shopper interest.

What does no impressions and no clicks mean?

No impressions and no clicks means a product is not being seen or clicked. This can point to weak product data, missing attributes, relevance issues, or visibility problems.

What is attribute completeness?

Attribute completeness shows whether important product data fields are present and complete. Missing or weak attributes can reduce visibility, relevance, and product matching.

Does the audit help with free listings as well as ads?

Yes. Feed quality can affect both paid Shopping activity and free listings because both depend on Google understanding product data accurately.
