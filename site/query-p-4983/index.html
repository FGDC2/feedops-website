Agency & Partner FAQ

How does FeedOps work with agencies?

We act as your feed team behind the scenes. You keep the client relationship; we handle the product data that powers campaign performance. Most agencies loop us in only when technical blockers arise—speeding delivery and building trust.

Will FeedOps replace or compete with our agency?

Never. We don’t run ads, manage budgets, or pitch strategy. We simply make your product data perform better—so your campaigns do too.

Can we involve FeedOps without giving up control?

Absolutely. You choose the visibility level. Some partners introduce us as part of their delivery team; others keep us fully white-label. Either way, your team stays in control.

Can FeedOps support multiple clients or an entire portfolio?

Yes. Whether you manage five clients or fifty, FeedOps provides centralised visibility, consistent delivery, and scalable support across every account.

Will FeedOps cause conflicts in the client’s tech stack?

No. FeedOps integrates cleanly with the ecommerce platform or data source and only publishes outbound feeds. We don’t touch the storefront or interfere with other systems.

Do you offer co-branded or white-labelled delivery?

Yes—typically for larger agency partners. We provide co-branded reporting, platform access, and private-label options where needed.

Can we access shared reporting or diagnostics?

Yes. Feed health diagnostics, campaign-level insights, and planning calls can be shared with you—or directly with the client, at your discretion.

What kind of support do agency partners receive?

Partners receive priority support, rapid diagnostics, shared planning sessions, onboarding help, and enablement resources to streamline delivery.

How do we get started as a partner?

Start with one client or campaign. We demo using their real product data, run a 30-day pilot to prove results, then formalise a partnership tailored to your portfolio.

Are there incentives or partner benefits?

Yes. While commercial terms remain private, partners get preferred support, early feature access, and other exclusive perks. If we’re a strong fit, we make collaboration worthwhile.
