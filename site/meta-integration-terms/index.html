Meta integration

Meta Integration Terms.

These terms apply when a user connects FeedOps to Meta Business, Facebook, catalogue, or related Meta account services.

Connection authorisation

By connecting FeedOps to Meta, you authorise FeedOps to access and use the Meta-related information required to provide the integration and related FeedOps services.

Authority to connect Meta accounts

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary authority to connect the relevant Meta Business, Facebook, or catalogue-related accounts to FeedOps.

Use of Meta integration data

FeedOps will use Meta integration data only for the purpose of providing, maintaining, supporting, and improving the Meta integration and related services.

For information about Meta-related data handling and deletion requests, see the Meta data deletion instructions.

Disconnecting the integration

You may disconnect the Meta integration at any time through FeedOps or your Meta account settings. Disconnecting the integration may limit or stop FeedOps' ability to provide Meta-related services.
