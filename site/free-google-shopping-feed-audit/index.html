FAQ

Questions about the Free Google Shopping Feed Audit.

Is the Free Google Shopping Feed Audit really free?

Yes. The FeedOps Google Shopping Feed Audit is free and automated. You can use it without becoming a FeedOps customer, replacing your feed provider, or committing to a paid service.

What does the Google Shopping Feed Audit check?

The audit checks Google Merchant Center product visibility, approval and compliance signals, attribute completeness, products with no impressions, zombie products with impressions but no clicks, and product data gaps that may limit paid Shopping and free listings.

What Google Merchant Center access does FeedOps need?

FeedOps needs read-only Google Merchant Center access with reporting and insights permissions. This allows the audit to read product data, approval information, impressions, and clicks without changing your feed or account settings. See exactly what FeedOps can access.

Does the audit change my feed or require Google Ads access?

No. The audit cannot change product feeds, prices, shipping settings, Merchant Center settings, campaigns, bids, or budgets. It does not require access to your Google Ads account.

How long does the Google Shopping Feed Audit take?

The automated audit begins after you enter your details and connect Google Merchant Center. Account-level findings are normally generated in minutes, although timing can vary with catalogue size and data availability.

What are zombie products and products with no impressions?

Zombie products receive Google Shopping impressions but no clicks. Products with no impressions are not being shown. Both can indicate product data, relevance, approval, or visibility issues that need investigation.

Does the audit cover paid Shopping and free listings?

Yes. The audit reviews Merchant Center product data and approval signals that can affect both paid Google Shopping activity and free listings. No Google Ads connection is required.

Who is the Free Google Shopping Feed Audit for?

The audit is designed for ecommerce teams, retailers, brands, and agencies that want to understand Google Shopping product visibility and feed health. Agencies can use it across multiple client Merchant Center accounts.

Can I rerun the audit or use it for multiple accounts?

Yes. You can rerun audits, review past results, and use the audit for multiple websites or Merchant Center accounts when access is configured for each account.

What happens after I submit the audit form?

After you submit your details, you connect the Merchant Center account you want audited and the automated analysis begins. FeedOps can help with access setup, and you can separately ask about support for fixing issues found by the audit.
