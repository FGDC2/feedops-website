Where to start

Pick the resource based on the problem in front of you.

FeedOps Learning is built for busy operators, marketers, and agencies. Start with the question you need answered, then move into the related guides once the basics are clear.

Fast paths

Use this route when you know the symptom but not the feed change behind it.

  • Products are disapproved or not showingStart with Merchant Center errors. Fix
  • The feed feels messy or underbuiltStart with Google Shopping feed optimisation. Guide
  • Campaigns need cleaner category logicStart with product type in Google Shopping. Explain
  • Search relevance and CTR are weakStart with product title optimisation. Improve

Learning themes

FeedOps Learning follows the same product data system we use in practice.

Feed quality

Titles, attributes, product types, categories, descriptions, images, pricing, and availability all shape Shopping visibility.

Merchant Center health

Errors and warnings are rarely isolated. They usually point back to missing, stale, mismatched, or poorly mapped product data.

Campaign structure

Better product data makes it easier to segment campaigns, compare categories, identify waste, and make product-level decisions.

AI discovery

Cleaner product data helps Google and AI shopping surfaces understand what each product is, who it suits, and when to show it.
