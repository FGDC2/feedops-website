FeedOps Learning

Learn to Make Your Product Data Your Best Sales Asset

These completely free, self-paced product feed courses prepare your product data  for agentic commerce while helping you master product feed optimisation. They use clear lessons and quick quizzes to track progress as you go. The goal is simple: turn marketers into product-data feed experts ready for what comes next.

Product Feeds & AI

1. Lesson - Introduction to Product Feeds

Objective: Build a foundational understanding of how product data connects your store to ad platforms and marketplaces. 

You’ll learn:

  1. What a product feed actually is
  2. How feeds act as the language between your store and ad platforms.
  3. Why feeds matter more today (AI-driven ad systems like PMax rely on feed quality).
  4. The difference between connected vs optimized feeds. 
  5. Common feed formats and standards (Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon).

Lesson Theme: What is a Product Data Feed

2. How Feeds Power eCommerce AI Advertising

Objective: Understand how product feeds fuel machine learning and targeting in ad platforms.

You’ll learn:

  • How AI learns from product attributes like title, price, color, and type.
  • Why data completeness and accuracy improve targeting.
  • How poor feeds waste ad spend (AI misfires).
  • How FeedOps aligns feeds with campaign goals like sales vs discovery. 
  • Example: How changing a product title impacts Performance Max results. 
  • Why Strong Website Data Drives Better Ads and Search Results

Lesson Theme: AI in eCommerce Ads

3. How Feeds Power Marketplaces

Objective: Learn how product feeds enable listings, search visibility, and ranking across eCommerce marketplaces.

You’ll learn:

  • How marketplaces like Amazon and eBay  feed data.
  • Difference between marketplace feeds and ad feeds. 
  • How syncing price, availability, and shipping improves buyer experience. 
  • Why taxonomy and attributes matter for filters and category placement. 
  • How FeedOps maintains consistent, optimized data across channels.

Lesson Theme: How Feeds Power Marketplaces

4. Anatomy of a Product Feed

Objective: Break down the essential components that make a product feed perform.

Core Components:

  • Identification: ID, Title, Description
  • Classification: Product Type, Google Product Category
  • Presentation: Image, Price, Availability, Condition 
  • Descriptive: Brand, GTIN, MPN, Material, Pattern, Gender, Size 
  • Optimization: Custom Labels, Promotions, Shipping, Local Inventory

Lesson Theme: Product Feed Components

5. From Raw Data to Optimized Feed

Objective: Show how your raw store data becomes an optimized, sales-ready feed.

Stages:

  • Raw Data: Pulled directly from your eCommerce platform.
  • Valid Feed: Meets channel requirements and passes error checks.
  • Optimized Feed: Titles and attributes aligned to campaign intent.
  • Sales-Ready Feed: Enriched for AI matching, remarketing, and ranking.
