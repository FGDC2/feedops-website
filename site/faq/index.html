Company questions

FeedOps company FAQs.

These answers explain what FeedOps is, who it helps, and why product feed optimisation is the centre of the business.

What is FeedOps?

FeedOps is a product feed optimisation and feed management platform for ecommerce teams. It helps improve product data for Google Shopping, Performance Max, free listings, marketplaces, paid social catalogues, and AI shopping discovery.

Who is FeedOps for?

FeedOps is for ecommerce teams, retail operators, agencies, performance marketers, and executives who need cleaner product data, stronger Merchant Center health, and better product-level visibility across shopping channels.

Is FeedOps a software company or a service company?

FeedOps is service-backed software. The platform gives teams tools to manage and improve product feeds, while the FeedOps team can help with audits, strategy, setup, troubleshooting, and ongoing feed optimisation.

Is FeedOps based in Australia?

FeedOps is Australia-first in its site, language, examples, and positioning. The platform and feed optimisation approach are relevant to ecommerce teams that sell through Google Shopping and other product data channels.

Platform questions

FeedOps platform FAQs.

These questions cover how the FeedOps platform fits into product feed work.

What does the FeedOps platform do?

The FeedOps platform helps audit, ingest, map, enrich, automate, sync, manage, and improve product feed data. It supports product title optimisation, product type cleanup, custom labels, feed rules, Merchant Center checks, and channel-ready product data workflows.

Does FeedOps replace my ecommerce platform?

No. FeedOps does not replace your ecommerce platform. It sits between your source product data and destinations such as Google Merchant Center, improving the quality and usefulness of the feed before it reaches advertising and commerce channels.

Can FeedOps help with product titles?

Yes. FeedOps can help improve Google Shopping product titles by applying title frameworks, feed rules, product attributes, and AI-assisted workflows where appropriate. The goal is to make titles clearer, more searchable, and more useful for Shopping relevance.

Does FeedOps use AI or LLMs?

FeedOps can use AI and LLM-assisted workflows for product data tasks such as title optimisation, product type assignment, product enrichment, and structured feed improvement. The focus is practical product data quality, not generic AI content.

What channels can FeedOps support?

FeedOps is centred on Google Shopping and Merchant Center work, and can support product feed improvement for Performance Max, free listings, marketplaces, paid social catalogues, affiliate destinations, and emerging AI shopping surfaces.

Audit questions

Free Google Shopping feed audit FAQs.

These answers explain how to use the audit as a starting point.

What is the free Google Shopping feed audit?

The free Google Shopping feed audit reviews Merchant Center and product feed health to identify missing attributes, weak product data, visibility gaps, zombie products, and issues that may reduce Google Shopping performance.

What does the audit check?

The audit looks for practical feed problems such as missing attributes, weak titles, product visibility gaps, no-impression products, category and product type issues, Merchant Center health signals, and data quality problems that can affect ads or free listings.

Do I need Merchant Center access for the audit?

Yes. The audit needs appropriate Merchant Center access so FeedOps can review product data and performance signals. The audit page explains the required access and what the process involves.

Should I start with the audit or a demo?

Start with the audit if you want a feed health snapshot first. Start with a demo if you already know the feed has commercial impact, multiple stakeholders, or urgent Merchant Center and Shopping performance issues.

How does FeedOps pricing work?

FeedOps pricing depends on feed complexity, catalogue size, channel requirements, support needs, and the level of feed strategy involved. The pricing page and demo help determine whether FeedOps is the right fit.

Is FeedOps priced for small catalogues or complex catalogues?

Can agencies use FeedOps?

Learning questions

FeedOps Learning FAQs.

FeedOps Learning is organised around practical questions, not generic blog categories.

What is FeedOps Learning?

FeedOps Learning is the resource hub for FeedOps guides, explainers, troubleshooting pages, and product feed education. It helps teams understand feed optimisation, Merchant Center issues, product types, Google Product Category, Shopping Graph readiness, free listings, and Shopping campaign workflows.

What is a shopping feed?

A shopping feed is a structured product data source that tells a shopping or advertising platform what products a retailer sells. For Google, the feed is submitted to Merchant Center and includes product titles, descriptions, prices, availability, images, identifiers, product types, categories, shipping, and landing page URLs. The shopping feed explainer breaks this down in more detail.

What is the difference between guides, explainers, and troubleshooting?

Guides are practical how-to resources. Explainers clarify an important concept such as the Google Shopping Graph, product_type, or Google Product Category. Troubleshooting pages help when something is broken, such as Google Merchant Center errors.

Which guide should I read first?

If you want the broadest starting point, read the Google Shopping Feed Optimization guide. If your issue is specific, start with the title optimisation guide, free listings guide, product type explainer, Google Product Category explainer, or Merchant Center errors page.

Does FeedOps Learning replace the platform?

No. FeedOps Learning helps teams understand the work, but the platform and FeedOps team help apply that work at scale inside real product data, Merchant Center, and channel workflows.

Troubleshooting questions

Merchant Center and feed troubleshooting FAQs.

These questions cover broken, rejected, or underperforming product data.

Can FeedOps help with Google Merchant Center errors?

Yes. FeedOps can help identify and fix many Merchant Center issues, including missing or invalid GTINs, price mismatch, availability mismatch, missing shipping, missing attributes, incorrect identifier_exists values, poor product titles, and weak category signals.

Can FeedOps help when Google Shopping ads are not showing?

Yes. FeedOps can help investigate whether products are not showing because of feed problems, disapprovals, low-quality product data, weak titles, missing identifiers, poor segmentation, campaign structure, or product-level performance issues.

Can FeedOps fix product type and Google Product Category issues?

Yes. FeedOps can help improve product_type values and check Google Product Category accuracy. Product type is usually a merchant-controlled classification signal, while Google Product Category is Google's taxonomy and is often auto-assigned by Google.
