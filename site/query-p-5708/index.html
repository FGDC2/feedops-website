What does the audit check?

The audit checks key product data signals that may affect Google Shopping performance, including visibility score, products with impressions but no clicks, products with no impressions, and attribute completeness.

What is Visibility Score?

Visibility Score helps you understand how visible your catalogue is likely to be across Google Shopping surfaces.

What are Zombie Products?

Zombie Products are products that get impressions but do not get clicks. They may be showing, but they are not attracting enough shopper interest.

What does No Impressions, No Clicks mean?

This highlights products that are not being seen at all. That can point to issues with product data, relevance, completeness, or overall visibility.

What is Attribute Completeness?

Attribute Completeness shows whether important product data fields are present and complete. Missing or weak attributes can reduce visibility and relevance.

Does the audit help with both paid and organic visibility?

Yes. The audit helps you identify issues that may affect both paid Shopping activity and free listings.

Will the audit tell me what is holding my product data back?

Yes. That is the purpose of the audit. It is designed to help you quickly identify weak signals, missing data, and missed opportunities.
