Google Shopping Feed Audit

What does FeedOps access for the audit?

The audit uses read-only Google Merchant Center access to review product feed health, product issue data, and reporting signals. It does not edit your feed, campaigns, pricing, shipping, or account settings.

Quick reassurance
  • Read-only audit access
  • No feed edits
  • No campaign edits
  • No Google Ads connection required
  • Disconnect anytime in Google Merchant Center

Access scope

FeedOps only needs the data required to diagnose feed health.

The audit is designed to show visibility gaps, product data issues, and Merchant Center signals that may be holding back Shopping and Performance Max results.

What FeedOps reads

  • Product feed diagnostics
  • Merchant Center issue and status data
  • Product-level reporting signals
  • Visibility, impression, and click patterns

What FeedOps does not change

  • Product feed content
  • Prices, stock, or shipping settings
  • Merchant Center account settings
  • Google Ads campaigns or budgets

Why reporting access helps

Reporting access lets the audit connect product data quality to performance signals, such as products with impressions but no clicks, products with no impressions, and visibility gaps.

You stay in control.

Access can be removed from Google Merchant Center at any time. The audit does not require a credit card, does not require Google Ads access, and does not commit you to becoming a FeedOps customer.
