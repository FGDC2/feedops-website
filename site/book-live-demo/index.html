Product feed demo FAQs

What is a product feed demo?

A short fit conversation where FeedOps reviews your product feed setup, channel blockers, catalogue data, and the next steps that may improve Shopping, PMax, Meta, marketplace, or feed-led performance.

Who should book one?

Ecommerce teams, executives, and media agencies dealing with messy catalogue data, product disapprovals, missing attributes, custom labels, pricing questions, or feed work that is slowing campaign growth.

Is it just a sales demo?

No. The call can include a short FeedOps platform walkthrough where it helps, but the main purpose is to understand the feed problem, commercial blocker, working model, and whether FeedOps is the right fit.
