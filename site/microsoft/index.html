Microsoft questions

Microsoft feeds: frequently asked questions

Do we need a Microsoft Advertising account?

Yes. Your business needs a Microsoft Advertising account and Microsoft Merchant Center store. FeedOps can help establish and configure them if they are not already in place.

Who adds the billing details?

Your business adds and controls its billing information directly in Microsoft Advertising. FeedOps can guide your team through the required steps.

Can we use the same feed as Google Merchant Center?

Microsoft and Google have similar product-data requirements, but their feeds should not be treated as identical. FeedOps uses your existing information and performance insights while preparing a separately controlled feed aligned with Microsoft’s requirements.

Can we send only selected products?

Yes. FeedOps can send the complete catalogue or select products using categories, brands, availability, price, margin, promotions, performance insights, custom labels and agreed rules.

Can our agency continue managing the campaigns?

Yes. Your agency can continue managing campaigns, budgets, bids and performance targets while FeedOps manages the product data and Microsoft Merchant Center feed.

What happens after launch?

FeedOps continues monitoring feed delivery, product eligibility, disapprovals and catalogue changes. We maintain rules, improve titles and attributes, investigate issues and keep product, price, availability and promotional data synchronised.

Where can Microsoft product ads appear?

Microsoft Merchant Center product data can support Microsoft Shopping, Performance Max and eligible feed-based Audience Ads and free product listings. Exact inventory depends on campaign type, settings, market and current Microsoft availability.
