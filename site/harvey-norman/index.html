Harvey Norman questions

Selling on Harvey Norman: frequently asked questions

What is Harvey Norman Customer Direct?

Customer Direct is Harvey Norman’s online partner programme. Approved partners can offer eligible products to Harvey Norman’s online audience across supported categories.

Can any retailer sell on Harvey Norman?

No. Businesses register their interest and Harvey Norman decides whether to approve the partnership. FeedOps can help assess and prepare your catalogue but cannot guarantee acceptance.

How does Harvey Norman rank online in Australia?

For August 2026, Similarweb estimated harveynorman.com.au at number 4 in Australia’s Consumer Electronics category, with 93.57% of its traffic coming from Australia.

Similarweb classifies Harvey Norman under Consumer Electronics rather than Marketplace. These third-party estimates change over time.

Does FeedOps integrate with Harvey Norman?

FeedOps can prepare and manage product data for Harvey Norman Customer Direct. The current connection method and supported catalogue, order and shipping workflows are confirmed during onboarding.

Can we send only part of our catalogue?

Yes. FeedOps can create inclusion rules based on agreed criteria such as category, brand, price, margin, availability and fulfilment capability, subject to Harvey Norman’s requirements.

Who controls prices and fulfilment?

Your business controls approved prices, promotions, inventory decisions and physical fulfilment. Exact responsibilities are governed by the partner agreement with Harvey Norman.

Does FeedOps guarantee approval or sales?

No. Harvey Norman controls partner acceptance, while sales depend on demand, pricing, competition, availability and fulfilment. FeedOps manages the product-data foundation but cannot guarantee approval or revenue.
