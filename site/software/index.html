Feed Audit

Start with a feed audit that measures visibility rate and highlights the metrics most likely to improve performance, including account compliance, product approval, and attribute completeness. Attribute completeness shows which missing or weak product attributes are limiting visibility, searchability, and discoverability.

Enrich with AI

Use custom AI agents to enrich missing variant information such as size, material, and colour, complete product categorization, and rewrite product titles. Each agent has its own AI settings, including language, target country, and attribute-specific instructions.

Variant enrichment Product types Long-tail keywords AI title rewriting

AI Settings

Custom agents for every brand and category.

FeedOps AI settings let teams customize how each agent enriches product data, so the output can match the brand, market, category, and commercial goal.

Fully customizable AI enrichment

Each custom agent can be tuned for brand voice, target country, language, category rules, attribute requirements, and specific instructions. If a large language model can interpret, rewrite, classify, extract, or enrich product data, FeedOps can help apply that work across the feed at scale.

Product Data

See and manage the product data each channel receives.

Product Data is the most universal part of FeedOps: simple enough to check what is available and in stock, powerful enough to manage product data at scale.

Visualize, edit, import, and export product data

Use Product Data to filter products, inspect what is being served to a specific channel, make single product edits, apply bulk edits, import updates, export data, and choose between manual or automated changes. Feed rules are one option, not the only way to improve the feed.

Channel views Bulk edits Single edits Import and export Manual edits Automated edits

Feed Rules

The automation engine for product feed logic.

Feed Rules turn repeatable feed decisions into automated conditions and actions across one or many channels.

Automate what changes, where it goes, and when it applies

Use Feed Rules to control what is in stock, what is excluded, what is sent to each channel, and how product data is augmented. Build title templates, apply structured categorization, and automate feed logic wherever a condition and action can describe the change.

Conditions and actions One or many channels Stock logic Channel exclusions Title templates Structured categorization

Orders

Track marketplace orders and unblock processing issues.

Orders gives ecommerce teams a clear view of current and recent marketplace orders, including what has processed, what is processing, and what needs attention.

See order status and fix mismatches

Use Orders to review current orders and the last 30 days of order activity, identify orders that have not processed, and find mismatches that may be stopping orders from flowing through. FeedOps gives teams tools to help diagnose, fix, and unblock order issues before they create operational drag.

Current orders 30-day order history Processing status Failed orders Mismatch fixes Marketplace order flow
