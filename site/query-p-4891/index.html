FeedOps Platform FAQ – Connect, Sync & Optimise

Integration & Compatibility

Which platforms does FeedOps support?

Native apps for Shopify and BigCommerce, plus API or flat-file connections for Magento 2, WooCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Neto, Ecwid, nopCommerce, Wix, and more.

Can FeedOps handle custom or headless ecommerce setups?

Yes. We build custom connectors via API or secure flat-file transfers—no need to rebuild your stack.

Does FeedOps support ERP or POS integrations?

Yes. Connectors are available for NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, Lightspeed, and other ERPs/POS with real-time or scheduled syncs.

Can FeedOps ingest data from flat files?

Absolutely. We accept CSV, TSV, XML, Google Sheets, and existing Merchant feeds. Updates can run on a schedule or be triggered manually.

Can we connect multiple data sources (store, ERP, POS)?

Yes. FeedOps merges and normalises data from every source into one clean master feed.

Will FeedOps cause conflicts in the client’s tech stack?

No. We integrate cleanly with your data sources and publish outbound feeds only—nothing touches the storefront or existing systems.

Syncing & Data Management

How long does onboarding take?

We start optimisation within 3–5 business days of kickoff. Most pilots go live in 10–15 days, depending on complexity and platform readiness.

How often does the feed sync?

Daily on Essential Concierge, up to near-real-time on Growth and Omni Concierge tiers. Manual sync buttons are always available.

How quickly do changes sync to FeedOps?

Price, stock, or content updates can sync every few minutes. On-change triggers keep shopper information accurate.

What happens if my schema changes or my store updates?

FeedOps handles API versioning, rate limits, and field mapping behind the scenes, so feeds keep running smoothly with minimal effort.

Will FeedOps overwrite or change my live product listings?

No. We create a separate, optimised feed for ads and marketplaces. Your storefront data stays untouched—use the improved data on-site only if you choose.

Supported Channels & Distribution

Do I need a separate feed for each channel?

No. FeedOps ingests your data once, then syndicates channel-perfect versions to Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, TikTok, Pinterest, and more.

Can FeedOps help with affiliate or partner feeds?

Yes. We deliver clean, up-to-date feeds for affiliates and comparison-shopping engines, ensuring brand, price, and availability consistency.

Can FeedOps support us on marketplaces like Amazon and eBay?

Yes. We format and enrich marketplace feeds to boost visibility, search rank, and buy-box performance, while updating inventory and shipping data to prevent oversells.

Does FeedOps handle order syncing from marketplaces?

Yes. Orders from Amazon, eBay, and other marketplaces sync back to your ecommerce platform automatically for streamlined fulfilment.

Core Platform Features

Will FeedOps replace our current feed tool or plugin?

Yes, if you choose. Unlike sync-only tools, FeedOps actively improves data quality, adds AI enrichment, and offers unlimited hands-on support.

How quickly can FeedOps improve product data?

AI-optimised titles, categories, and attributes are generated as soon as the first sync completes—often within hours for Shopify and BigCommerce users.

Compliance & Error Handling

How does FeedOps ensure data accuracy?

We validate and normalise every attribute, map fields to each channel, auto-fix errors, and enrich missing data before feeds go live.

Does FeedOps flag and fix compliance issues?

Yes. Auto-healing resolves most errors; your Concierge alerts you only when human input is required—cutting disapproved items by up to 92 % month-over-month.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Hosted on AWS with annual third-party penetration tests and GDPR/CCPA compliance.
