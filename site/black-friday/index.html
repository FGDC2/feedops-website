Frequently asked questions

Black Friday promotion FAQs.

Is this offer available outside Australia?

No. This three-service Black Friday promotion is exclusively for eligible Australian retailers. Businesses outside Australia are not eligible for this promotional package.

How long does the audit take?

Most feed audits are completed within a few business days after the initial strategy session and once FeedOps has the access or product data needed for the review. More complex catalogues may require additional time.

Do I need to become a FeedOps customer?

No. You do not need to be an existing FeedOps customer or agree to become one. The eligible free services are provided without a subscription, contract or purchase obligation. FeedOps may explain relevant paid services if further work would help, but choosing them is optional.

Are there any obligations?

No. The strategy session, feed audit and any eligible setup services are provided without obligation. You can use the findings and decide what to do next.

Can I claim all three offers?

Eligible Australian retailers may qualify for one, two or all three services. The number depends on your current Google Shopping setup, Merchant Center access, product catalogue and whether you operate physical stores.

Which services require physical stores?

Free Local Listings Setup and Local Inventory Ads Feed Setup require physical retail locations in Australia and store-level product availability. The Google Shopping Feed Audit is available to eligible Australian online retailers without physical stores.

What access will FeedOps need?

The exact access depends on the service. FeedOps may need appropriate access to Google Merchant Center, your product feed and, for local services, store and local inventory data. The team will confirm what is required during the strategy session.

Does the offer include advertising spend or campaign management?

No. The offer covers the eligible feed audit and setup services listed on this page. Google Ads spend, ongoing campaign management and work outside the agreed setup scope are not included.

What happens after I book?

Write “Black Friday” in the booking message field so the FeedOps specialist knows you are interested in this promotion. The team will review your retail setup during the strategy session, confirm which services you are eligible for and explain the information or access needed.

How long is the promotion available?

The promotion is available to eligible Australian retailers while FeedOps has onboarding capacity. Availability is limited, so booking early gives the team more time to complete and validate work before peak demand.
