FAQ

Questions ecommerce teams ask about FeedOps

Clear answers about the FeedOps platform, managed service, onboarding and ongoing support.

What does FeedOps manage for us?

FeedOps manages the work required to keep your product data accurate, enriched and ready for each channel. This includes source connections, field mappings, transformation rules, titles, attributes, taxonomy, Custom Labels, feed monitoring, product disapprovals and ongoing optimisation.

Is FeedOps software or a managed service?

It is both. FeedOps combines a proprietary platform, custom AI agents and a dedicated specialist team. You receive platform access for visibility, collaboration and approvals, while FeedOps takes responsibility for day-to-day feed operations.

Will FeedOps replace our ecommerce platform, ERP or PIM?

No. Your existing systems remain the authoritative sources for product, pricing, inventory, order and fulfilment data. FeedOps operates between those systems and your advertising, marketplace and shopping channels, transforming source data into channel-ready outputs.

Can FeedOps handle large or complex catalogues?

Yes. FeedOps currently manages more than 12 million active products and variants, with its largest managed catalogue exceeding 2.5 million SKUs. We also support complex variants, configurable products, bundles, modular ranges and catalogues assembled from multiple suppliers.

Which channels does FeedOps support?

FeedOps supports major advertising, social, marketplace, affiliate, local inventory and AI shopping destinations. These include Google Shopping, Performance Max, Microsoft Advertising, Meta, Pinterest, TikTok, Criteo, Amazon, eBay, Rakuten and ChatGPT. Custom destinations can also be assessed and developed.

Who handles feed errors and product disapprovals?

FeedOps does. Our team proactively monitors feed health, delivery issues, missing attributes, catalogue errors and product disapprovals. We resolve issues within our control and coordinate with your team, agency or technology provider when the cause sits in another system.

How long does onboarding take?

Initial implementation and validation generally takes between two and four weeks. FeedOps manages the technical discovery, connections, mappings, rules, feed construction, testing and channel setup. Your team primarily provides access, business requirements and approvals.

Can FeedOps work alongside our existing agencies?

Yes. FeedOps acts as the specialist data and feed layer supporting your paid media, SEO, social and marketplace partners. Agencies can request labels, promotion IDs, product segments and channel-specific changes without having to maintain the underlying feed infrastructure.
