Quick answers

Product feed optimisation in plain terms.

Product feed optimisation improves ecommerce product data so shopping channels, marketplaces, paid social platforms, local inventory systems, and AI discovery surfaces can better understand, approve, match, and display products.

What is product feed optimisation?

Product feed optimisation is the process of improving titles, attributes, categories, labels, availability, and other product data so products perform better across feed-based channels.

Why does product feed optimisation matter?

Better product feeds help channels understand what each product is, reduce preventable errors, improve eligibility, and give campaigns cleaner data for matching, testing, and scaling.

How does FeedOps improve product feeds?

FeedOps improves product feeds with audits, feed rules, AI enrichment, expert support, and ongoing product feed management across Google Shopping, PMax, paid social, marketplaces, and AI discovery.

Product feed optimisation FAQ

What buyers need to know before choosing a feed partner.

FeedOps is built for teams that need more than feed translation software. The goal is healthier feed operations, better product data, stronger channel readiness, and practical performance improvements across media channels and marketplaces.

What is product feed optimisation?

Product feed optimisation is the process of improving the product data sent to media channels, shopping platforms, and marketplaces so products can be better understood, matched, displayed, and clicked.

How is FeedOps different from feed translation software?

Feed translation software mainly moves data from one format to another. FeedOps combines software, automated updates, feed rules, feed audits, custom AI enrichment, structured experiments, and live experts who actively manage and improve the feed.

Which product data can FeedOps improve?

FeedOps can improve product titles, product types, product highlights, product details, attributes, colours, sizes, patterns, variants, missing fields, and category-specific content.

Does the process work across channels and marketplaces?

Yes. FeedOps applies the same audit, enrich, test, and scale process across media channels and marketplaces, with the optimisation customised for each channel, brand, market, and product category.

Can FeedOps help with feed errors and disapprovals?

FeedOps audits product feeds to identify faults, missing attributes, rejected items, disapprovals, weak content, sync issues, and channel health problems, then helps prioritise and apply the right fixes.

What performance signals does FeedOps focus on?

FeedOps focuses on practical feed performance signals such as product visibility, impressions per product, clicks per product, CTR, channel readiness, fewer rejected items, and richer product data.

How does this help a PPC or PMax manager?

FeedOps improves the product data that Shopping, PMax, paid social, and marketplace campaigns depend on: titles, product types, attributes, custom labels, approval health, and feed structure. The goal is to give campaigns cleaner data to match, test, and scale from.

What does a head of media get from FeedOps?

Media leaders get a clearer operational layer behind campaign performance. FeedOps helps connect feed quality, approval issues, catalogue readiness, and channel-specific data work to the media outcomes their teams are accountable for.

What does a head of ecommerce need to know?

FeedOps supports the ecommerce workflow around catalogue data, product approvals, variants, marketplaces, sync issues, and team ownership. It can be used by internal teams, alongside an agency, or as a managed support layer when feed work needs more accountability.

Does FeedOps support normal feed management work?

Yes. FeedOps supports operational feed management work such as automated updates, feed rules, mappings, product approval support, error management, and software-led order sync alerts and fixes where channels require them.

Can FeedOps help keep orders and shipping data synced?

FeedOps can support the feed-related data flows around orders and shipping information for channels and marketplaces that require operational sync as part of their feed management process.

Do customers get access to the FeedOps platform?

Yes. FeedOps is not a black-box service. Customers get access to the platform and can choose to use it themselves, work with guided support, or ask FeedOps experts to manage the work for them.

Can FeedOps do the feed work for us?

Yes. FeedOps can work as a guided platform, a collaborative feed management system, or a done-for-you service depending on how much your team wants to own internally.
