Biggest gaps

    Suggested cleanup rules

    • Assign one primary keyword per indexable page. Secondary terms can live in headings, FAQs, and supporting copy.
    • Noindex admin, cart, checkout, author archives, category archives, thank-you pages, and duplicate archive pages unless there is a deliberate SEO reason.
    • Consolidate duplicate posts that target the same query, especially product feed optimisation, Google Shopping feed, PMax, and Merchant Center topics.
    • Use the redesigned pages as the source of truth for core commercial keywords.
    • Ask whether each new indexable page belongs in the global footer before marking the page complete.
